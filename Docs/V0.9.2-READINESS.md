# v0.9.2 readiness

21 September 2026. A re-audit of the seven areas that decide whether the beta is worth
handing to friends, after the first-run work in this pass.

Labels: **[LIVE]** watched happening, **[MEAS]** a number off this machine, **[VERIFIED]**
read from the code or an artefact, **[INFER]** follows but was not observed,
**[NOT VERIFIED]** not established.

## Verdict

**Friend testing: GO.** **Wider public beta: GO, with two download-page sentences.**
**v1.0: NO-GO**, unchanged and for unchanged reasons.

## Remaining blockers

### Blocking v1.0 — not the beta

1. **The manual QA matrix is unrun.** Scaling other than 200%, more than one monitor, mixed
   DPI, sleep and wake, Windows 10 1809, and x64 installed anywhere. **[NOT VERIFIED]**, all
   of it. This is what the beta is for.
2. **Nothing is signed.** `SIGNPATH-SUBMISSION.md` is written and unsent.
3. **The pre-release flag must be revisited** before stable users exist.
4. **Uninstall leaves the run-at-login entry**, now on every machine.

### Blocking the beta

**None.**

## Friend-testing readiness

**Ready.** Three clicks and eighteen seconds from installer to a library of charms, no tray
icon required, the app stays running, and nothing can lose anybody's data. The two things
that will cost testers are SmartScreen and picking the wrong architecture, and both are
explained in `TESTER-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

## Public beta readiness

**Ready, with two sentences on the download page** — neither in the code:

1. **"Almost certainly the x64 one"** next to the x64 download. The current guidance sends
   people three clicks into Settings to answer a question about an ornament, and the wrong
   choice runs slowly rather than failing, so nobody finds out they chose wrong.
2. **A line about what the SmartScreen warning is**, above the fold rather than in a linked
   document.

## Recommended next milestone

**Hand out the beta and wait.** Nothing on the open list can be closed from this machine:
the QA matrix needs other people's hardware, SignPath needs somebody else's calendar, and
whether the Ropes switch and the Create tab are discoverable needs somebody who has not
read the source.

The work worth doing while waiting, in order:

1. **Send the SignPath application.** Longest lead time in the project, still unsent.
2. **Remove the run-at-login entry on uninstall.** An hour, and it now affects everybody.
3. **Link `PRIVACY.md` from the About page.** The document is thorough and invisible from
   inside the app.
4. **Say somewhere that the wrong architecture runs slowly.** One sentence, no code.

**Do not start v1.0 work, Creator Studio, or anything on the v1.1 list until testers have
reported.** The point of a beta is to find out which of the guesses in
`Docs/BETA-TESTER-AUDIT.md` were right.
