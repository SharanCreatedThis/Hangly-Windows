# Code of Conduct

Hangly is a small project. This is a short document because a long one would be pretending
there is a committee.

## The standard

**Be decent to people.** Assume the person you are replying to is doing their best with
less context than you have, because they usually are.

That means: no harassment, no personal attacks, no demeaning or discriminatory comments,
no unwelcome attention, and no publishing anyone's private information. It also means the
smaller things — not being contemptuous about someone's question, their English, their
hardware, or the fact that they did not read the whole README.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages in this repository, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Email **swarnsharan@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and are not shared
further. If a report concerns the maintainer, say so plainly — you will get a straight
answer rather than a process.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts, and will say
why when it happens.
